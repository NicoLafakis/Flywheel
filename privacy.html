Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

The short version

Flywheel has no accounts, no sign-in, no email address, no password, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere unless you choose to put a score on the public board.

What stays on your device

Your progress lives in your browser's local storage and never leaves it. That includes your unlocked cities, coin bank, per-city records, audio and control settings, and the display name you have claimed, if any.

Clearing your browser's storage for this site erases all of it. There is no backup and no recovery, because there is no account for us to restore it from.

What is sent when you post a score

Posting a score to the public board is a deliberate action. Only then does anything leave your device, and only the following:

  • A display name you choose. You pick it; we do not derive it from anything.
  • A random device key. Your browser generates 24 random bytes the first time the board is used. It is not derived from your hardware, your browser configuration, or anything else about you. It is a random number, and it is not a fingerprint. It exists so a single browser cannot flood the board.
  • A recording of the run. Every input you made during the run, plus the score you are claiming, the run's statistics, and which build of the game you played. The server replays the run to confirm the score is real. This is the whole reason the board can be trusted without asking anyone to sign in.
  • A name token. When you claim a name, the server generates a secret and sends it to you once. We store only its SHA-256 hash. We cannot read the token back out, and we cannot recover it for you if you lose it.

Your IP address

We do not store your IP address. When a request reaches the server, the address is put through a keyed cryptographic hash and truncated, and only that digest is kept, to limit how often one connection can submit. The digest cannot be turned back into your address.

What is public

If you claim a name, that name appears on the public scoreboard next to your verified score and the run's statistics. Nothing else about you is published.

Removing yourself

The PROFILE screen has a remove control. It deletes your player record and the name it holds. You can also simply clear this site's browser storage, which removes the local save and the name token from your device.

Who else handles this data

  • Vercel hosts the site and runs the server functions.
  • Supabase provides the database that holds the board.

There is no one else. No analytics provider, no advertising network, no error tracker, no third-party script of any kind.

Children

Flywheel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the game asks for no personal information at all, there is nothing for a child to disclose beyond a display name they choose.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact
