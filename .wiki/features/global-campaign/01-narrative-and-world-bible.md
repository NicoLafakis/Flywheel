# 01 — Narrative & World Bible: The Shattered Marketing Engine & Sprocket’s Odyssey

## 1. Character Lore & Creative North Star

### The Mark, The Void & The Drive Gear
**Sprocket** is a sentient, titanium-alloy drive gear with a frictionless gravitational singularity at its center. 

Unlike traditional destructive monsters or apocalyptic voids, Sprocket is an engine of **kinetic momentum and preservation**. In mechanical engineering and business growth theory, the **Flywheel Effect** demonstrates that true breakthrough power does not come from a single explosive jolt or a fragmented funnel, but from compounding, frictionless revolutions where every turn builds on the last.

* **The Outer Toothed Rim (12 Sprocket Teeth):** High-speed milling edges that fracture and digest architectural matter into clean modular voxels.
* **The Central Core (The Hole):** A microscopic gravitational event horizon that converts consumed mass directly into **rotational momentum**.
* **The Protagonist Identity:** Sprocket is the liberated **Central Drive Wheel** of the mythical **Universal Marketing Machine**.

## 2. The Overarching Story: The Shattered Marketing Engine

### The Fall of the Machine
In the legacy corporate era, the ultimate engine of organic growth—**The Universal Marketing Flywheel**—was dismantled. Broken apart by organizational silos, bloated agency retainers, fragmented funnels, and bureaucratic inertia, the machine's 29 vital mechanical components were scattered across the globe.

Corporate conglomerates, high-frequency trading firms, media networks, and legacy agencies locked these master components deep inside their most fortified buildings—penthouse boardrooms, subterranean bank vaults, broadcast spires, and tech incubators across 29 world metropolises.

### Sprocket’s Global Liberation Tour
Sprocket is on a worldwide mission to retrieve every single missing module:
1. **Infiltrate & Scout:** Sprocket deploys to each metropolis, scanning the skyline for the "oddball" architectural landmark holding that city's trapped module.
2. **Chew & Collapse:** Sprocket feeds on street props and mid-rises to build mass to Size 4–5, chews through the target structure's corner bays and load-bearing columns, and collapses the building to crack open its core.
3. **Extract & Socket:** The glowing **Marketing Engine Module** drops from the rubble into Sprocket's vortex, permanently locking into the player's master blueprint board ("Reverse Operation").
4. **The Cambridge UNBOUND Finale:** Across 7 Acts and 29 cities, the machine is reassembled piece by piece. At HubSpot Global HQ in Cambridge, MA during the UNBOUND Summit, Sprocket docks into the central hub, igniting the perpetual growth flywheel in a blinding kinetic celebration!

## 4. In-Level Gameplay & "Where's Waldo" Target Buildings

### Architectural Contrast & Visual Tells
Each city features exactly **one target building** housing the city's hidden marketing module. The clue system relies on the player's keen eye:
* **Early Acts (Prologue – Act I):** Obvious scale/color contrast (e.g. a stubby 2-story red-brick diner nestled between uniform 30-story gray towers).
* **Mid Acts (Acts II – IV):** Architectural flourishes (e.g. unique copper dome rooflines, bay windows, or distinct historic facades among modern glass boxes).
* **Late Acts (Acts V – VII):** Subtle "Where's Waldo" structural quirks (e.g. non-standard stepped terraces, offset helipads, or slight footprint rotations).

### The Carmen Sandiego Briefing Intel System
Before launching a run, the **Mission Dossier** provides an intercepted intel clue that gets progressively more cryptic across the campaign:
1. **Early Acts (Plain English):** *"Intel confirms the Inbound Antenna is secured in the bright-red corner diner by the south wharves."*
2. **Mid Acts (Corporate Memos & Telegraphs):** *"Intercepted cable: Target component moved to 4th-floor agency loft with green copper roof overlooking the eastern canal bend."*
3. **Late Acts & Megacities (Local Lore & Riddles):** *"Follow where the elevated rails bend south past the only pre-war brick facade with an unlit clockface."*

## 5. The Sub-60s Speedrun Extraction Bonus

To reward elite routing and observation, every city features an **Early Extraction Window**:
* **The 60-Second Clock:** When the level begins, a golden 60-second countdown runs in the HUD.
* **The Speedrun Loop:** The player scans the skyline, speed-eats a line of props to reach Size 4–5, chews the corners of the target building, collapses it, and swallows the module within the first 60 seconds.
* **The 2× Payout:** Securing the module in $\le 60\text{s}$ **DOUBLES (2×)** the entire final level coin payout upon completion!
* **100% Full-Clear Gate:** The player must complete the 100% full clear to officially bank the part into the Master Machine (preventing cheese quits).
* **Replay Farming:** On subsequent runs of a cleared city, the module slot is already filled, but the Sub-60s 2× coin bonus remains active as a lucrative farming challenge.

## 6. The 7 Acts of the Global Campaign

1. **Prologue: Calibration (The Lab)** — Sprocket awakens; installs the *Drive Hub Spindle*.
2. **Act I: The Pacific Awakening (Sydney, Auckland, Singapore)** — Maritime testing; gathering the *Inbound Collection Hoppers* and *Oceanic Antennas*.
3. **Act II: Asian Megacities & High-Density Grids (Hong Kong, Seoul, Tokyo, Beijing, Bangkok, Mumbai)** — High-frequency attention grids; securing the *Searchlight Beacons* and *Attention Escapements*.
4. **Act III: Desert Horizons & Mediterranean Antiquity (Dubai, Cairo, Athens, Rome)** — Cracking limestone vaults and ancient monoliths; recovering the *Qualification Sifters* and *Pricing Elasticity Pistons*.
5. **Act IV: European Capitals of Grandeur (Paris, London, Amsterdam, Berlin)** — Touring financial and agency capitals; unlocking the *Deal Closer Press* and *Brand Polish Buffers*.
6. **Act V: The Americas & Transcontinental Transit (Rio, Buenos Aires, Mexico City, San Francisco, Chicago, Toronto)** — Mastering steep grade physics and transit loops; capturing the *Customer Delight Dynamos* and *Referral Feedback Loops*.
7. **Act VI: The New York Megacity Trilogy (Lower Manhattan, Brooklyn, Upper Manhattan)** — Conquering 140,000+ voxels; securing the *Multi-Touch Attribution Meters* and *ICP Targeting Scopes*.
8. **Act VII: The Massachusetts Tech Corridor & Grand Finale (Boston Seaport, Cambridge UNBOUND Summit)** — The final march; docking into the fully assembled **Universal Marketing Flywheel** atop HubSpot Global HQ in Cambridge!
