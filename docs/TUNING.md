# Tuning & the beatability proof

## Growth curve

`playerRadius(mass) = clamp(0.45 + 0.115 * mass^0.42, 0.45, 6.0)`

Tier unlock thresholds (mass needed for `playerRadius > tierRadius`):

| Tier | Radius | Mass to unlock | Cumulative mass in a typical level at unlock |
|------|--------|----------------|----------------------------------------------|
| 1 | 0.35 | 0 (start 0.45 > 0.35) | 0 |
| 2 | 0.47 | ~1 | snack ring |
| 3 | 0.64 | ~30 | first street sweep |
| 4 | 0.86 | ~120 | first block |
| 5 | 1.16 | ~330 | first parking lot |
| 6 | 1.57 | ~760 | mid district |
| 7 | 2.12 | ~1500 | late district |

## The proof (tools/validate.mjs)

For each of the 100 levels, the validator:

1. Rebuilds the city from the level seed (same code as the game).
2. **Overlap check**: pairwise via spatial hash; `dist(c1,c2) >= r1 + r2 + 0.05`.
3. **Snack-ring check**: ≥10 edible objects within 6 m of spawn; simulated
   time-to-first-eat < 1.0 s.
4. **Greedy bot**: each step moves toward the centroid of the nearest cluster of
   currently-edible objects (respecting tide bounds, landmark shield, rivals
   eating the same food). Fixed 60 Hz steps, real movement speed curve
   `speed = max(4.5, 7.5 - 0.35*radius)` so growth costs maneuverability.
5. Asserts `massBot >= target` with `timeLeft >= 0.15 * clock`.
6. Asserts `target >= 0.55 * massBotEstimate` (target not trivially low) and
   `target <= 0.75 * massBotEstimate` (headroom for human imperfection).

The bot is deliberately dumb (greedy, no path planning around buildings); a human
routing even slightly better clears with more margin. If the greedy bot passes
the 15% margin gate, the level is declared provably beatable.

Targets in `levels.js` were generated by running the bot once and freezing
`floor(0.62 * botMass)`; the validator re-runs and enforces the margin, so any
regression in citygen or tuning fails loudly.

## Difficulty ramp

- Clock: 75 s (L1) → 150 s (L100), +3–6 s per level within a metro, jump at
  metro boundaries.
- District size: 90×90 m → 170×170 m.
- Prop density and tier mix shift upward per metro (more tier 4–7 mass later).
- Rivals: 1 (metro 2) → 3 (metro 5), rival speed 0.8× → 0.95× player speed.
- Tides: 1 shrink event → 3 events, final bounds ≥ 55% of original area.
- Landmark: unlockMass 400 → 1200, reward 500 → 2500 mass.
